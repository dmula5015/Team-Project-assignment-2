About Student Planner

A simple way to stay organized during the semester.

Why I Made This Website

Keeping up with several classes, assignments, tests, and due dates can be difficult. I created College Student Planner so students can keep their schoolwork in one place instead of trying to remember everything.

What You Can Do

Add your classes, enter assignments with due dates, check upcoming deadlines, and mark assignments complete. The dashboard gives you a quick view of your week.

How It Works

This draft uses HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. Information is stored in your browser using localStorage, so it stays available on the same browser after refreshing. It is not synced across devices. React is planned for a later stage.
